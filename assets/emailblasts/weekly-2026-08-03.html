Three opening-shift essentials
Make the first walkthrough count.
Brooms and brushes
Match the tool to the surface: softer bristles collect fine dust on smooth indoor floors, while stiffer bristles are better suited to heavier debris and rougher areas.
Keeping the right broom near each work zone makes quick touch-ups easier before traffic starts.
Shop brooms and brushes
 
Cleaners and degreasers
Cleaner performance depends on more than a quick spray. Following the label's dilution and contact-time directions gives the product time to loosen soil instead of leaving the whole job to extra scrubbing.
Check bottles, concentrates, and dispensing supplies together so the opening crew has a complete setup.
Shop cleaners and degreasers
 
Entrance and work mats
Mats work best when they lie flat and stay clear of built-up soil. A quick opening inspection can catch curled edges, shifting, or saturation before foot traffic turns a small issue into a larger one.
Rotate, clean, or replace tired mats so entrances and standing work areas begin the month ready.
