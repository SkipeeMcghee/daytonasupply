Pack Smarter This Fall
A smooth shipping station starts before the first carton is packed. Match the box, cushioning, and tape to the job now so your crew can move through fall orders with fewer supply swaps and less searching at the bench.
Stock the packing bench
Three parts of a better pack
Start with the package, then fill the gaps.
Step one
Choose a right-sized box
Measure the product and leave only the room needed for suitable cushioning. A box that is too small can crowd the item, while excess empty space takes more fill and gives contents more room to shift.
Keep your most-used carton sizes close to the bench and record their item numbers for faster reordering.
Shop corrugated boxes
Cushion the empty space
Wrap delicate surfaces and fill open areas so contents do not move freely. Keep the cushioning type and roll size your crew uses most within easy reach.
Shop bubble products
Finish every seam
For an H-style seal, tape the center seam and both edge seams on the box flaps. Check tape width, adhesive, and dispenser fit before ordering the next case.
