First stop
Paper products
Count the rolls, towels, and folded stock at each shared station before building the refill list. Note the product format and size so the replacement fits the dispenser or storage space already in use.
Share the product name, size, or item number if you need help matching the current supply or finding a suitable alternative.

Soap and sanitizer
